Whispera 1.4 is the biggest update so far. It adds the dictation controls, text clean-up and history that people switching from other dictation apps have asked for, a second speech engine, optional LLM post-processing, a redesigned Settings window, and Spanish, German and French translations. Transcription still runs on your Mac.

### Dictation control

- **Choose how the shortcut works**: press to toggle, push-to-talk (record while the key is held), or hold-or-toggle, which does both depending on how long you hold. A modifier key on its own can be the shortcut.
- **Cancel a recording** with Escape, or rebind the cancel key to something else. You can also cancel from the recording pill.
- **Keep capturing briefly after you stop**, so the last word is not cut off.
- **Microphone options**: open the microphone per recording, keep it open for a short while, or keep it always on. Pick the input channel on multi-channel interfaces, use a separate microphone when the lid is closed, and fall back to the default microphone if yours disconnects.
- **Free memory when idle**: unload the model after a timeout you choose (from immediately up to an hour) or on demand. It loads again the next time you dictate.
- Optional: mute system audio while recording, set the feedback sound volume and output device, or use your own sounds.

### Accuracy and language

- **Custom words** with fuzzy correction, so names and jargon come out spelled the way you want.
- **Filler-word removal** drops "um", "uh" and similar words from transcripts.
- **Automatic language detection**, or pick the spoken language yourself.
- **Simplified and Traditional Chinese conversion** for Chinese output.
- **Skip silence** with voice activity detection. Choose the energy-based detector or the neural Silero model. Clips with no speech are dropped instead of producing stray text.

### Post-processing

- Rewrite transcripts with your own prompts after dictation using an LLM. Supported providers are OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Groq, Cerebras, Z.AI, AWS Bedrock, Apple Intelligence (on-device, macOS 26) and any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, including local servers such as Ollama or LM Studio.
- Post-processing has its own shortcut (Option-Shift-Space by default), so you can choose per dictation whether to clean up the text.
- It is off until you turn it on. API keys are stored in the Keychain.

### History

- A searchable history of your dictations, with the original and post-processed text side by side.
- Star entries you want to keep, retry a transcription or re-run post-processing, copy, or delete.
- Saving the audio of each dictation is off by default. Turn it on to replay or re-transcribe recordings.
- Choose how long history is kept: the latest entries only, 3 days, 2 weeks, 3 months, or forever. History can be turned off entirely.
- The menu bar menu has History and Copy Last buttons.

### Models

- **Parakeet** (NVIDIA Parakeet TDT v2 for English and v3 for 25 European languages, through FluidAudio) is available as an alternative to Whisper.
- **Import your own models** from Hugging Face or a local folder.
- **Choose the accelerator** a model runs on: Automatic, Neural Engine, GPU or CPU.
- While a new model loads, Whispera shows which model is handling your dictation in the meantime.

### Text insertion

- Choose how transcripts are inserted: paste, type, copy only, or hand the text to a script.
- Optionally restore your clipboard after pasting, add a trailing space, or press Return after inserting.
- Paste delays can be adjusted for apps that need more time.

### Interface

- **Redesigned Settings** with a sidebar in place of tabs, so every section stays reachable at any window size and in every language.
- **Recording overlay** with a screen-edge glow while you dictate, and a choice of overlay position.
- **Light, Dark or System** appearance, independent of the macOS setting.
- **Hide the menu bar icon** if you prefer to work from the shortcut alone.
- After an update, Whispera shows what changed once.

### Localization

- Whispera is now available in Spanish, German and French, in addition to English.

### Security and privacy

- Transcripts and history stay on your Mac. The network is used only to download models, check for updates, and for post-processing when you choose a cloud provider.
- Secure Input is respected: Whispera tells you when a password field or another app blocks the dictation shortcut, keeps a fallback shortcut working, and never keeps text typed into a secure field on the clipboard or in history.
- Script-based text insertion only runs a script you approved by choosing it in Settings. If the script changes, it has to be approved again.

### Automation

For those who want it, Whispera can also be driven from outside the app: `whispera://` links, a command line on the app binary, Shortcuts actions, and Raycast script commands. Remote control through links is off until you turn it on; the README explains how to set these up.

### Distribution

### Fixes

- Many reliability, performance and security fixes across recording, model loading, text insertion and the menu bar.
