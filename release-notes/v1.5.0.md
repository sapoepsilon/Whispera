Whispera 1.5 lets you keep an eye on the coding agents running in herdr on your Mac from your iPhone, adds speech server and streaming engines, turns post-processing into Recipes, moves Settings to the native macOS layout, keeps a recording you cancel so you can still transcribe it, and makes onboarding lighter and smoother. On-device transcription is still the default.

### Control your herdr agents from your iPhone

- **Pair your iPhone with your Mac** through the Whispera iOS app. Sign in on both, then confirm the new device on the Mac with Touch ID and a safety number. Devices you already trust can vouch for a new one.
- **Encrypted connections only.** Your iPhone connects to the Mac directly over Tailscale, or through your account's relay, which encrypts every message end to end, when it can't reach the Mac directly. Notifications reach the phone when an agent needs you. Plain, unencrypted local network connections are refused unless you turn on "Allow unencrypted local network connections" in Settings, and the Mac is only advertised on the local network while that setting is on.
- **Answer agents from the phone**: approve or deny permission prompts, reply to questions and fill in forms an agent asks you to complete, and approve requests for secrets on the Mac.
- **See what agents are doing**: follow their conversation and subagents, watch a live terminal for herdr, herdr-core and tmux panes, review repository diffs, and browse an agent's workspace files read-only. Files an agent mentions open as previews.
- **Send work to agents**: dictate on the phone and have your Mac transcribe it with the engine you selected in Whispera, attach images and other files, and use your saved recipes.
- **herdr-core support**: terminals and agents running in herdr-core (`hc`) are listed and can be driven next to the ones in herdr and tmux.
- Whispera runs a small helper in the background for this, and its status is shown in Settings.

### Speech servers and streaming

- **New transcription engines**: send dictation to your own OpenAI-compatible speech server, to Whisper through your Whispera account, or to a provider with your own API key, in addition to the on-device models.
- **Automatic engine**, which picks the best available path and falls back to on-device transcription when a server can't be reached.
- **Streaming dictation** from a realtime speech server shows your words live while you speak and pastes the transcript once, when you stop.
- **Final pass**: optionally polish a stopped dictation with a second, more accurate Whisper pass.
- When a server is slow, refuses the connection or fails, Whispera says what happened instead of failing silently.

### Recipes

- Post-processing is now part of **Recipes**, as the built-in Clean up recipe. Recipes stay off until you turn them on.
- Recipes can run automatically on a matching dictation before it is pasted, and the pill shows when one is running.
- A **Try it** row runs Clean up on text you type, just like a dictation would.

### Settings

- Settings uses the native macOS layout, with grouped forms.
- A new **Servers** pane gathers the speech and LLM server setup in one place, including server checks and the list of models a server offers.

### Cancelled recordings are kept

- Cancelling a recording with Escape (or from the pill) no longer throws the audio away. Whispera keeps the last 10 cancelled recordings from the past 24 hours.
- They are listed in the menu bar menu, where you can transcribe one with your selected engine, which copies the text and adds it to History, or delete it.
- Very short clips and anything recorded while Secure Input was on are not kept, and the recordings are readable only by your user account.
- Turn off "Keep cancelled recordings" in Settings > History to stop keeping them; turning it off also deletes the ones already kept.

### Onboarding

- New particle transitions between onboarding steps and when dictated text lands in the app you are typing into.
- Onboarding uses much less CPU and energy while it is open, and stops using any once it closes.
- Animations respect the Reduce Motion setting.

### Under the hood

- Every library Whispera depends on now ships inside the project, so builds no longer fetch packages from the internet.

### Fixes

- The menu bar menu closes when you open Settings instead of staying on top of it.
- Many smaller reliability and performance fixes.
